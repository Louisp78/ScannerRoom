# ScannerRoom — Brand identity

## Name

**ScannerRoom**

Inspired by the scanner room building in *Subnautica*: a module that maps and reveals what surrounds it underwater. The brand evokes exploration, discovery and bringing the seabed to light.

## Motto

> **Explore the coast, one dive at a time.**

*(French alternative: « Explorez les côtes, plongée après plongée. »)*

## Colour palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Abyss blue** (primary) | Deep ocean blue | `#0A2E44` |
| **Scanner cyan** (accent) | Bright turquoise | `#2CD3D9` |
| **Foam white** (neutral) | Light off-white | `#F2F7F7` |

**Rationale:** abyss blue sets depth and seriousness (seabed, scientific grounding), scanner cyan evokes the light beam and the technological / revealing side, foam white keeps things readable and airy.

## Brand tone

Clear, curious, approachable. At the crossroads of the rigorous scientist and the passionate explorer — serious about data, amazed by the ocean.
